How I shoot

About the work

Photography, for me, is systems thinking with a shutter. Every frame is a decision about what to include, exclude, and hold in tension. The camera is a tool for making arguments visible.

The full archive lives at PhotographyX — a dedicated space for the visual work, separate from the systems and computational writing here. It is updated regularly with new series and single frames.
